Data Privacy Notice

Barangay Poblacion, Bani, Pangasinan

e-Poblacion collects and processes personal information to deliver barangay services such as resident records, document requests, certifications, and community programs.

  • Full name
  • Address and household details
  • Contact information
  • Valid identification documents
  • Selfie or identity verification information

Your personal data is collected only for legitimate barangay purposes and is handled in accordance with applicable Philippine data privacy requirements, including the Data Privacy Act of 2012.

You have the right to be informed, to access, and to request the correction of your personal data.

This notice is provided for general information and does not constitute a certified legal compliance statement.
